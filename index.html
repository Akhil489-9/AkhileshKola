What I work on

Four kinds of work, one process: understand the design, model it, detail every part, and hand the shop something it can cut and install without guessing.

Architectural millwork

Curved booths, lattice ceilings, feature walls and lobby interiors for offices, hotels and landmark buildings, including a stadium monument torch and observation-deck ceilings.

Store fixtures & retail

Gondolas, cash desks, back walls, display cubes and pop-up stores, built as modular fixtures that ship flat and go up fast.

Experiential marketing

Immersive brand spaces, VR stations, holiday installations and event stages: pieces people walk into, not just look at.

Trade show exhibits

Island booths, demo stations, monitor towers, ramps and hanging signs for major tech, retail, energy, security, healthcare and hospitality shows across North America.
